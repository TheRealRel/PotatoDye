Potato Dye changes how your gear looks: dye your cape at the barber, and put the weapons, tools, shield and quiver on your back where you like them. It changes looks only.

**Dye your cape at the barber.** Sit in the barber's chair and a Cape color panel opens beside the hair settings, with three sliders. Your cape changes color as you move them.

- **Hue** picks the color from a rainbow strip.
- **Saturation** is how strong the dye is: at 0 the cape keeps its own colors.
- **Brightness** makes it lighter or darker.
- **Apply** keeps the color, **Cancel** puts it back, and **No dye** returns the cape to its own colors.

Dyeing is free, like changing a hair style. Colors are saved with your character, one per kind of cape: dye your Linen cape red and your Wolf cape blue, and each shows its own color whenever you wear it. It dyes the cape you see, so with Vanity Outfit showing a different cape, that's the one you dye. The color tints the cape's own texture, so light capes (Linen) take it best and dark, furry ones (Wolf, Lox) only shift a little.

**Move what's on your back.** Hold Right Ctrl and nudge the weapon, tool or shield on your back, or Better Archery's quiver, into the spot you like:

| Keys, holding Right Ctrl | What they do |
|---|---|
| Arrow keys | Move it up, down, left or right |
| Page Up / Page Down | Move it away from or closer to your back |
| Right Shift with any of those | Tilt it instead |
| Home | Pick the next thing on your back |
| End | Put it back where the game puts it |

Each press moves it about 1 cm or tilts it 3 degrees, and holding a key repeats. Every kind of item keeps its own place, saved with your character, so all your Iron Swords sit where you put yours. The quiver's place goes into Better Archery's own quiver settings.

**Other players with Potato Dye see your colors and places.** Players without it see the normal look, and nothing breaks either way.

Part of the **PotatoServer** pack for the Potato Server.

## Settings

In `BepInEx/config/potato.dye.cfg`, or in game with Configuration Manager. All of them are personal.

| Setting | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Show cape colors | On | Show dyed capes in their colors, yours and other players'. |
| Barber panel | On | Show the Cape color panel at the barber. |
| Holster places | On | Show weapons, tools and shields on the back where their owners put them, and let you move yours. |
| Holster adjust key | Right Ctrl | The key to hold while moving what's on your back. |
